-	Example in the image is a regional hometown association for those from the Chinese province of Fujian.
-	Chinese cultural associations are groups that often form around a Clan, a region or a group of people to serve as a place to make connections.
-	These places acts as third spaces for members of the community, often used to pray or play mahjong.
-	Cultural Associations have accumulated many properties in the Chinatown area. 
-	They provide for the needs of their group, whether it be housing, religious space or a cultural centre.
