Title: Toronto's First Chinatown
Text:
-	The first Chinatown started to take shape in the early 1900s as hundreds of Chinese men settled close to Union Station after helping to build the Canadian Pacific Railway across Canada.
-	In the late 1950’s, construction of New City Hall resulted in the full expropriation and destruction of the first Chinatown
-	This caused mass displacement of Chinatown’s original residents, forcing them to relocate, and splitting them between the new (West) Chinatown and East Chinatown.
