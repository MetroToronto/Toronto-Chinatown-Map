Title: Spotlight: Mao House
Text:
-	Owned by a younger Chinese woman, recently moved into the mall.
-	Says that the low rates in the mall have provided a real space for creatives to move further from digital platforms.
-	Over the last two years, many fashion and art focused stores have opened in Chinatown Centre.
-	Rent has gone up in the past two years, raising fears that the mall management is seeking to gentrify and displace businesses.
