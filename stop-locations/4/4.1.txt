-	Kensington Market sits on the northwestern corner of Chinatown. Many Chinese businesses are located within, creating a blended area between the two neighbourhoods.
-	Kensington Market has seen more widespread gentrification than Chinatown, becoming mostly tourism focused.
-	This has resulted in the loss and replacement of greengrocers, multicultural stores and the increase of rent prices.
-	The Kensington Market Community Land Trust has made protecting multiculturalism, fighting large development and preserving and expanding affordable housing its top priorities.
